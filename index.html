About me

Hi, I'm Jordi de Leon Chapuis. I started learning programming when I enrolled in the Multimedia degree at UOC, which I'm currently studying. Since then I've built several projects from scratch. One of my proudest achievements is building a website for a local Swiss bakery I handled everything myself: design, multimedia, development and deployment. It's not a huge thing, but for me it's a very important step forward.

I try to learn something new every day. I'm currently training at the mouredev.pro campus, deepening my knowledge of different programming languages with a growing interest in JavaScript and Python.

I'm also very interested in how AI is being integrated into everyday work. I use tools like Claude Code to build real projects, but always under my own judgment using AI as an ally, not letting it do everything for me.

My goal is to work as a front-end developer at a company where I can keep improving my skills, learn more and become a better programmer every day.
